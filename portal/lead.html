What’s included

Project scope

  • Removal and disposal of countertops, backsplash, range hood, cabinets and sink
  • New cabinets with soft-close doors and drawers
  • Vent hood and ducting
  • Tile backsplash with dimmable under-cabinet lighting
  • New countertops and a spacious sink

    How this works

    Every option the client chooses on a selection board shows up here with its vendor, quantity and your cost, grouped by vendor. Mark items Ordered (with the order #) and then Received. Attach a receipt when it isn’t Home Depot or Lowe’s. Each afternoon, Claude adds newly ordered items to this job’s Cost Detail workbook (Purchases tab). Set an approximate start date at the top (or add “Construction start” on Key dates) and everything shows an order-by date about 6 weeks before it.

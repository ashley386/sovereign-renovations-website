Our story

Sovereign Renovations

Though only started in 2021, Sovereign Renovations has been an idea in the making for a decade. We (Greg and Ashley Sovereign) spent 17 years working with high school and college students before moving back to the Triangle during COVID. Each of our homes over the years served as playgrounds and blank canvases to create, update, reconfigure, and completely overhaul to suit our changing needs and simply beautify our living spaces. DIY was the weekend and summer hobby, but the undertakings grew larger and more involved. Friends and family members asked for our time and talents and the additional work became a new challenge to dream bigger. From basements to bathrooms, laundry rooms to living rooms, and kitchens to closets, we have become experts in turning mere ideas into completely transformed spaces.

Since returning to the Triangle in 2020 and beginning Sovereign Renovations, the specialty projects have grown to include custom cabinets, mantels, range hoods, feature walls, ceiling/lighting features, and much more. With a focus on quality craftsmanship, doing business with integrity, and hearing our clients' needs and ideas, Sovereign Renovations has grown to be a highly-regarded design+build solution in the Southern Wake County area.

The family behind the work

Sovereign Family

We have always made a great team. From our college days serving together as class officers to our newlywed days volunteering as church youth leaders, our skills and gifts have always complemented each other. Over the past two decades our gifts have dovetailed to build a beautiful home and family, scores of completed renovation designs and projects, and happy homeowners ready to make their new spaces well-lived and well-loved.

Your collaboration with us is an opportunity for us to bring our gifts together for something beautiful and functional. It is a cherished invitation to deal honorably, listen patiently, and work tirelessly to bring ideas to life. This is our promise to you– that you would experience care, quality, and character from the initial consultation through the final walk-through.
